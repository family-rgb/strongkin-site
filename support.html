Common questions

How do I pair my child’s device?

Install StrongKin on your child’s device and choose the child option. On your own phone, approve the pairing request. Your child will then see their own simple view.

My child recorded a reply but it hasn’t arrived.

If their device was offline, the reply is saved and will send automatically when it’s back on Wi-Fi or data. Open the app on their device once it’s connected.

I’m not getting notifications.

Make sure notifications are allowed for StrongKin. iPhone: Settings → Notifications → StrongKin. Android: Settings → Apps → StrongKin → Notifications.

The app can’t use my microphone.

Allow microphone access for StrongKin. iPhone: Settings → StrongKin → Microphone. Android: Settings → Apps → StrongKin → Permissions → Microphone.

How do I move to a new phone?

Sign in with the same account on your new phone and your messages, letters and memories will sync back.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Account settings → Delete account. See our privacy policy for what is removed.
