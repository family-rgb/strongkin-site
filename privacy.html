Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

StrongKin (“we”, “us”) makes a family app for sharing voice messages, letters, photos and memories. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Website updates. If you leave your email on our website so we can tell you when StrongKin is ready, we store that email for that purpose.
  • Account information. When you sign up we store your email address (and name, if you add one) through our authentication provider, Supabase, so you can sign in and recover your account.
  • Your family’s content. Voice recordings, replies, journal letters (including drafts and scheduled letters), family stories, memory cards, photo albums, profile photos, family dates such as birthdays, and questions you submit. These are stored in your StrongKin account so they sync across your devices and reach the family members you choose.
  • Microphone, camera and photos. We only use the microphone when you choose to record, and the camera or photo library when you choose to add a picture.
  • Push notification tokens. If you allow notifications, we store a device token so we can tell you when a new message or reply arrives.
  • Crash reports. We use Sentry to receive reports when the app crashes or hits an error (device type, app version, and technical details). It is configured not to send personal information by default.
  • Usage analytics. We use PostHog to understand which features are used so we can improve the app (for example, “a message was recorded”). We don’t send the contents of your messages, letters or photos to analytics.
  • Purchases. If you make an in-app purchase, payment is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), depending on your device. We receive your purchase status (via our subscription provider, RevenueCat), never your card details.

If you use the app without an account, your content stays on your device until you create one. Some preferences (like theme) always stay on the device.

How we use it

  • To provide the app: store, sync and deliver your messages to your family.
  • To send notifications you’ve allowed.
  • To fix bugs and improve StrongKin.
  • To manage subscriptions.

We do not sell your information, show ads, or use your family’s content to advertise to you.

Who can see your content

Messages and memories are shared only within your household and with households you choose to connect. Journal entries are private to their author unless you share them. Our service providers (Supabase for storage and sign-in, Sentry, PostHog, RevenueCat, Apple, Google) process data only to run the service for us.

Children

StrongKin is set up and managed by a parent or guardian. Children use a simplified view on a device paired with a parent’s approval; they don’t create their own public profiles and can’t be contacted by strangers. Parents can review and delete their children’s content.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your account from Profile → Account settings in the app. When you do, StrongKin is cleared from that phone, you are removed from your household right away, and we send a confirmation to your email. Your account is permanently deleted 30 days later.

Changed your mind? Sign back in with the same account within those 30 days and your account and household membership are restored.

When your account is permanently deleted, we delete your sign-in, your profile and profile photo, your private journal, and other things you added under your account, such as memory cards, weekly answers and your voice on album photos. Voice messages you already left for your family stay available to them. If no one else is left in a household, that household and everything in it are deleted too. Other household members keep their own accounts.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with access controls so only your family can reach it. No system is perfect, but we work to protect your information.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data. Contact us and we’ll help.

Changes

If we change this policy we’ll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
