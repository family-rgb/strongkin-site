What is StrongKin

The things you mean to say, in the voice they know.

Busy days go quickly, and family is often spread out. StrongKin gives your whole family — near or far, young or old — a calm place to say the small things and the big ones — a good-luck message before the game, a story from when you were little, a “proud of you” they can play again and again.

It’s private, it’s just for your family, and it’s made to feel as gentle as a note left on the kitchen table.

Features

Everything a family needs to stay close

Nothing noisy. Just the pieces that help you show up for each other.

🎙️

Voice messages & replies

Record a message in your own voice — Grandma with a bedtime story, a cousin with a birthday hello, a kid with news from school. Kids can send a voice reply right back, too. Back-and-forth, like a conversation that keeps.

🕯️

A weekly family moment

Each week brings one simple question or activity to do together — a dinner thank-you, a walk, a family recipe. An uncle can answer it too. Small rituals, shared across the family.

🧸

Open When messages

Record messages for the moments you can’t be there: when they’re scared, when they miss you, before a big test. They open them when they need them.

📖

Family stories, in parts

Tell the longer stories — how Grandpa met Grandma, where the family came from — one part at a time, so they’re easy to record and easy to listen to.

✉️

Journal letters

Write a letter to anyone in the family — a niece heading off to college, a cousin far away. Save drafts, schedule it for later, and sign off your own way.

📚

Memory books & albums

Gather photos and moments into family albums and memory books — holidays, pets, recipes, summers worth keeping.

🎂

Birthdays & Coming up

StrongKin keeps the family’s important dates close, so you can have a birthday message ready before the day arrives.

🔒

A kid-safe device

Pair a child’s phone or tablet with a parent’s approval. Kids get a simple, gentle view made just for them.

📶

Replies that wait for Wi-Fi

No signal? Kids can still record a reply. It sends on its own once they’re back online.

How it works

Three small steps

  1. Set up your family

    Download StrongKin, create your account and invite your family with an invite code — grandparents, siblings, aunts, uncles, cousins. Have kids? Add them and pair their device with your approval.

  2. Record your voice

    Leave a message, answer this week’s family moment, save an Open When message for later, or record a future message for a day still to come.

  3. Listen and reply

    Your family hears your voice and can reply right back. Kids listen and reply in their own simple view.

Questions

Frequently asked

Who is StrongKin for?

Anyone who’s family. Parents and kids, grandparents, aunts and uncles, cousins, nieces and nephews — StrongKin is one place for all of you to send voice messages, share stories and stay close to each other, whoever’s talking and whoever’s listening.

Is my family’s information private?

Your messages, photos and letters are stored in your StrongKin account and shared only with the family members you connect. We don’t sell your data and we don’t show ads. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Is it safe for kids?

Children use a separate, simplified view. A child’s device is paired to the family only with a parent’s approval, and there are no public profiles, feeds, or messages from strangers.

Does it work on Android?

Yes. StrongKin is coming soon to both iPhone (App Store) and Android (Google Play).

Can grandparents, aunts and uncles use it?

Yes. StrongKin is for the whole family, not just parents. Grandparents, aunts, uncles and cousins can be part of your family in the app to send messages, letters and stories, and hear the kids’ replies.

What if we don’t have internet?

Replies recorded offline are saved on the device and sent automatically once you’re connected again.
